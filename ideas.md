# Darklines Shipping Website — Design Direction

## Selected Approach: Industrial Cartography

Darklines is presented as a confident, tactile logistics partner through a folded-shipping-chart visual language. The page uses warm paper surfaces, ink navy authority, burnt sienna action accents, sage teal capability cues, editorial serif headlines, manifest labels, route lines, port nodes, and asymmetric service sequencing.

### Core Experience

The homepage includes a contact strip, Darklines navigation, an auto-rotating sea/air/road hero carousel, company introduction, service route manifest, operating capabilities, fieldwork image band, enquiry CTA, and the supplied company address, email, and phone number.

### Motion Rules

The visual system now shifts to carbon black, bone white, and signal red. The hero now layers a muted, looping maritime video behind the carousel stills with an image poster fallback. The unique scroll interaction is a cargo-route spine: a red line grows as the visitor moves through the page, checkpoint dots activate at About, Services, Capabilities, and Enquiry, and each section receives a restrained lift/fade reveal. The route remains informative rather than decorative, and reduced-motion users see the same content without movement.

### Black-and-Red Style Decisions

The hero carousel auto-rotates every 6.5 seconds, pauses while hovered, and exposes previous/next controls plus slide indicators. Route lines draw in once, cards lift slightly on hover, and reduced-motion preferences disable non-essential animation. The red scroll spine is the recurring signature motion across the page.
